Millions of people have diabetes or prediabetes and don't know it. 🩺

In my NextGen Institute ML internship, our team built a screening tool: answer a few questions, get a risk level and a clear "see a doctor" signal.

• Trained and tuned 4 models on 229,474 real CDC survey responses
• Our best model catches 81.2% of people with prediabetes/diabetes (ROC-AUC 0.82)
• Just 7 questions keep 99% of the full model's performance
• Biggest lesson: accuracy was misleading (85% of people are healthy). Recall and threshold choice were what mattered, because a missed patient costs more than an extra blood test.

⚠️ It's a screening aid, not a diagnosis.
